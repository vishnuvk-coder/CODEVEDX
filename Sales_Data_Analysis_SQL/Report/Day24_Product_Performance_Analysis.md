# Day 24 – Advanced Product Performance & Profitability Analysis

## Objective

The objective of Day 24 was to perform advanced product-level sales analysis using SQL and identify the products contributing most significantly to overall business revenue.

## Analysis Performed

- Product sales performance analysis
- Product revenue ranking
- Product units-sold ranking
- Revenue contribution analysis
- Top-performing product identification
- Low-performing product identification
- Product order frequency analysis
- Average revenue per order
- Product performance classification
- Advanced business reporting

## SQL Concepts Used

- SELECT
- JOIN
- GROUP BY
- Aggregate Functions
- Common Table Expressions (CTEs)
- Window Functions
- RANK()
- DENSE_RANK()
- SUM() OVER()
- CASE expressions
- ORDER BY
- LIMIT
- Subqueries
- Percentage calculations

## Key KPIs

The analysis focused on:

- Total Revenue
- Units Sold
- Total Orders
- Revenue Rank
- Revenue Contribution %
- Average Revenue per Order
- Product Performance Category

## Business Insights

The analysis helps identify:

1. Products generating the highest revenue.
2. Products selling the highest number of units.
3. Products contributing significantly to total revenue.
4. Low-performing products requiring further investigation.
5. Products with high order frequency.
6. Products with strong average revenue per order.
7. High-performing and low-performing products.

## Business Recommendations

- Prioritize high-revenue products in sales strategies.
- Monitor low-performing products.
- Analyze customer demand for top-selling products.
- Maintain sufficient inventory for high-performing products.
- Consider promotional strategies for underperforming products.
- Use product revenue contribution to support business planning.

## Outcome

Successfully completed Day 24 of the Sales Data Analysis project.

The project now includes advanced product-level analytics using SQL aggregation, CTEs, window functions, ranking, revenue contribution analysis, and business performance classification.
