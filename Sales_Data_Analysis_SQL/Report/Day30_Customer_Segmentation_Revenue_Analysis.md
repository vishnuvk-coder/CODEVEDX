## 🎯 Objective

The objective of Day 30 is to analyze customer purchasing behavior and revenue contribution to create meaningful customer segments.

The analysis combines customer order frequency, units purchased, revenue, average order value, revenue ranking, and revenue contribution to support business-oriented customer segmentation.

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### 1. Customer Purchase Summary

Calculated the total number of orders, total units purchased, and total revenue generated by each customer.

### 2. Average Order Value

Calculated the average revenue generated per order for each customer.

### 3. Customer Revenue Ranking

Ranked customers according to their total revenue contribution.

### 4. Revenue Contribution

Calculated the percentage contribution of every customer to overall sales revenue.

### 5. Customer Segmentation

Customers were classified into:

* High Value
* Medium Value
* Low Value

based on their total revenue.

### 6. Segment Distribution

Analyzed the number of customers and total revenue generated by each customer segment.

### 7. Purchase Frequency Analysis

Customers were classified according to their number of orders:

* Frequent Customer
* Regular Customer
* Occasional Customer

### 8. Top 20% Customers

Used `NTILE(5)` to identify the top revenue group representing approximately the top 20% of customers.

### 9. Final Customer Business Report

Combined customer purchasing behavior and revenue metrics into a single business-oriented report.
