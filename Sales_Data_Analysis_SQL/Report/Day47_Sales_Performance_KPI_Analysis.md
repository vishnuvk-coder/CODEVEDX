# 📊 Day 47 — Sales Performance KPI Analysis

## Introduction

Day 47 focuses on **Sales Performance KPI Analysis** using MySQL.

The objective of this analysis is to convert sales transaction data into important **business performance indicators (KPIs)**. These KPIs help understand overall sales performance, customer activity, order behavior, revenue generation, monthly trends, and product contribution.

This analysis uses the existing `sales_analysis_db` database and demonstrates practical SQL techniques that can be used in **Data Analyst, Business Analyst, and Business Intelligence** roles.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of Day 47 are:

* Calculate total orders.
* Calculate total customers.
* Calculate total products.
* Calculate total revenue.
* Calculate total units sold.
* Calculate Average Order Value (AOV).
* Calculate average units purchased per order.
* Calculate revenue per customer.
* Calculate orders per customer.
* Analyze monthly sales performance.
* Measure customer revenue contribution.
* Measure product revenue contribution.
* Create an overall sales KPI summary.

---

## 🗄️ Database Used

```text
Database: sales_analysis_db
```

### Tables Used

```text
orders
order_items
products
customers
```

### Important Revenue Calculation

Revenue is calculated using:

```text
order_items.quantity × products.price
```

The `order_items` table does not contain the product price, so the `products` table is joined to obtain the price.

---

# 📌 Analyses Performed

## 1. Total Orders

The first analysis calculates the total number of orders available in the sales database.

### Business Purpose

This KPI measures the overall order volume generated by the business.

---

## 2. Total Customers

The second analysis calculates the total number of customers.

### Business Purpose

This provides an overview of the size of the customer base.

---

## 3. Total Products

The third analysis calculates the total number of products available in the product database.

### Business Purpose

This helps understand the size of the product catalog.

---

## 4. Total Revenue

The fourth analysis calculates total revenue using:

```text
Quantity × Product Price
```

### Business Purpose

Total revenue is one of the most important sales KPIs because it represents the overall value generated from recorded sales transactions.

---

## 5. Total Units Sold

This analysis calculates the total quantity of products sold.

### Business Purpose

Revenue alone does not show sales volume. Total units sold provides an additional measurement of product demand.

---

## 6. Average Order Value

Average Order Value (AOV) measures the average revenue generated by each order.

The calculation is performed by first calculating revenue for every order and then calculating the average.

### Business Purpose

AOV helps understand the typical monetary value of an individual customer order.

---

## 7. Average Units per Order

This analysis calculates the average number of products purchased in each order.

### Business Purpose

This KPI helps understand customer purchasing volume at the order level.

---

## 8. Revenue per Customer

Revenue per customer is calculated by dividing total revenue by the number of distinct customers.

### Business Purpose

This KPI provides an overall view of the revenue generated relative to the customer base.

---

## 9. Orders per Customer

This analysis calculates the average number of orders placed per customer.

### Business Purpose

It helps measure customer purchasing frequency at an overall business level.

---

## 10. Monthly Sales Performance

Monthly sales performance combines multiple KPIs:

* Total orders
* Total units sold
* Total revenue
* Average Order Value

The results are grouped by month using the order date.

### Business Purpose

Monthly analysis makes it possible to observe changes in sales activity over time.

It can help identify:

* Higher-sales months
* Lower-sales months
* Changes in order volume
* Changes in units sold
* Changes in average order value

---

## 11. Customer Revenue Contribution

This analysis calculates revenue generated by each customer and their percentage contribution to total revenue.

### Metrics

```text
Customer Revenue
Revenue Contribution Percentage
```

### Business Purpose

This analysis helps identify how revenue is distributed across the customer base.

It can be used for:

* Customer segmentation
* Revenue concentration analysis
* Customer value analysis
* Business reporting

---

## 12. Product Revenue Contribution

This analysis calculates revenue generated by each product and its percentage contribution to total revenue.

### Metrics

```text
Product Revenue
Revenue Contribution Percentage
```

### Business Purpose

This provides a product-level view of sales performance.

It can help identify:

* High-revenue products
* Low-revenue products
* Product revenue concentration
* Products contributing significantly to overall sales

---

# 📊 KPI Summary

The final KPI query combines important business measurements into one result.

The summary includes:

| KPI                 | Description                           |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Total Orders        | Total number of recorded orders       |
| Total Customers     | Total number of customers             |

This provides a compact view of the overall sales performance of the business.

# 🧠 SQL Concepts Used

Day 47 demonstrates several important SQL concepts:

### Aggregate Functions

```sql
COUNT()
SUM()
AVG()
```

Used for calculating business KPIs.

### JOIN

```sql
JOIN
```

Used to combine:

* Orders
* Order Items
* Products

### GROUP BY

Used for:

* Monthly analysis
* Customer analysis
* Product analysis
* Order-level calculations

Used to arrange customer and product revenue from highest to lowest.

Used when calculating unique customers and orders.

Used to group sales data by month.

Used to make financial KPI results easier to read.
