# Day 25 – Sales & Profitability Analysis

## Objective

The objective of Day 25 was to perform advanced sales and profitability-oriented analysis using MySQL.

The analysis focuses on product performance, revenue generation, customer revenue, order value, sales contribution, and business KPIs.

## Analysis Performed

- Product sales performance
- Product-wise quantity sold
- Product-wise revenue
- Average product price
- Highest-priced products
- Lowest-priced products
- Order-level revenue
- Customer revenue
- Customer average order value
- Overall sales KPIs
- Top products by revenue
- Top products by units sold
- Product revenue contribution
- Products generating above-average revenue
- Monthly sales performance
- Daily sales performance
- Product revenue ranking
- Product performance classification
- Overall business summary

## SQL Concepts Used

- SELECT
- JOIN
- GROUP BY
- HAVING
- ORDER BY
- COUNT()
- SUM()
- AVG()
- ROUND()
- DATE()
- DATE_FORMAT()
- CASE
- Subqueries
- Common Table Expressions (CTEs)
- Window Functions
- RANK()

## Business KPIs

The analysis calculates:

- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Total Units Sold
- Total Revenue
- Average Order Value
- Product Revenue
- Product Sales Contribution
- Customer Revenue

## Business Insights

The analysis helps identify:

1. Top-performing products.
2. Products generating the highest revenue.
3. Products with the highest sales volume.
4. Customers generating the most revenue.
5. Average revenue generated per order.
6. Products contributing significantly to overall revenue.
7. Monthly and daily sales trends.
8. High, medium, and low-performing products.

## Outcome

Day 25 strengthened practical SQL skills for business-oriented sales analysis and demonstrated how SQL can be used to convert transactional sales data into actionable business insights.
