# 📊 Day 56 — Customer Revenue Contribution & Concentration Analysis

## 📌 Overview

**Day 56** focuses on analyzing **customer revenue contribution and revenue concentration** using SQL.

The objective of this analysis is to understand how much revenue each customer generates, what percentage of total business revenue each customer contributes, how customers rank by revenue, and how concentrated the overall revenue is among the highest-value customers.

This analysis extends the customer analytics work completed in previous days and provides a business-oriented view of customer revenue distribution.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of Day 56 are:

* Calculate total revenue generated by each customer.
* Calculate overall business revenue.
* Measure each customer's percentage contribution to total revenue.
* Rank customers according to revenue.
* Calculate cumulative revenue contribution.
* Identify the top 10 revenue-generating customers.
* Measure revenue generated by the top 20% of customers.
* Classify customers based on their revenue contribution.
* Identify high-value revenue customers.
* Identify low-value revenue customers.
* Analyze revenue concentration across the customer base.
* Create a final customer revenue contribution summary.

---

# 📊 Day 56 Analyses

## 1. Total Revenue per Customer

This analysis calculates the total revenue generated by every customer.

Revenue is calculated using:

```text
Revenue = Quantity × Product Price
```

The calculation uses:

* `orders`
* `order_items`
* `products`

The result provides a customer-level revenue view.

### Business Use

This helps identify customers who generate the highest amount of revenue.

---

## 2. Overall Business Revenue

This analysis calculates the total revenue generated across all orders.

### Business Use

The overall revenue value provides the baseline required to calculate each customer's percentage contribution to the business.

---

## 3. Customer Revenue Contribution Percentage

This analysis calculates the percentage of total business revenue contributed by each customer.

### Formula

```text
Customer Revenue Contribution %
=
Customer Revenue / Overall Revenue × 100
```

### Business Use

This helps understand the relative importance of individual customers to total revenue.

---

## 4. Customer Revenue Ranking

Customers are ranked according to their total revenue.

The analysis uses the SQL window function:

```sql
RANK() OVER (ORDER BY total_revenue DESC)
```

### Business Use

Revenue ranking makes it easier to identify the highest-revenue customers and compare customers based on their contribution.

---

## 5. Cumulative Revenue Contribution Percentage

This analysis calculates the cumulative percentage of total revenue as customers are ordered from highest to lowest revenue.

### Business Use

Cumulative revenue contribution helps identify how quickly the business's total revenue is accumulated among its highest-value customers.

---

## 6. Top 10 Customers by Revenue

This analysis identifies the ten customers with the highest total revenue.

### Business Use

The results can support:

* VIP customer identification
* Customer relationship management
* Retention planning
* High-value customer analysis
* Revenue-focused business strategies

---

## 7. Top 20% Customers' Revenue Contribution

This analysis identifies the top 20% of customers according to revenue and calculates how much total revenue they contribute.

The SQL analysis uses:

* `ROW_NUMBER()`
* `COUNT() OVER()`
* `CEIL()`
* Revenue aggregation

### Business Use

This provides a practical way to understand whether a relatively small group of customers accounts for a large share of total revenue.

---

## 8. Customer Revenue Contribution Classification

Customers are classified according to their percentage contribution to total revenue.

### Classification Framework

| Revenue Contribution | Classification                |
| -------------------: | ----------------------------- |
|          10% or more | Very High Revenue Contributor |
|             5%–9.99% | High Revenue Contributor      |
|             2%–4.99% | Moderate Revenue Contributor  |
|             1%–1.99% | Low Revenue Contributor       |
|             Below 1% | Very Low Revenue Contributor  |

### Business Use

This classification makes customer revenue contribution easier to interpret and segment.

---

## 9. High-Value Revenue Customers

Customers generating **₹10,000 or more** in total revenue are identified as high-value revenue customers.

### Business Use

This segment can be used for:

* High-value customer monitoring
* Retention analysis
* Customer relationship management
* Revenue protection
* Targeted business analysis

---

## 10. Low-Value Revenue Customers

Customers generating **less than ₹1,000** in total revenue are identified as low-value revenue customers.

### Business Use

This segment helps identify customers with relatively low revenue contribution and provides a basis for further customer behavior analysis.

---

## 11. Revenue Concentration Analysis

This analysis combines customer ranking, revenue contribution, and cumulative revenue contribution.

Each customer is assigned a revenue concentration segment based on cumulative revenue.

### Classification

| Cumulative Revenue | Segment                        |
| -----------------: | ------------------------------ |
|          Up to 20% | Top Revenue Concentration      |
|          Up to 50% | High Revenue Concentration     |
|          Up to 80% | Moderate Revenue Concentration |
|          Above 80% | Long-Tail Revenue              |

### Business Use

Revenue concentration analysis helps understand how total business revenue is distributed across customers.

---

## 12. Final Customer Revenue Contribution Summary

The final analysis combines the major customer revenue metrics into one summary.

The output includes:

* Customer ID
* Total orders
* Total units purchased
* Total revenue
* Revenue contribution percentage
* Revenue rank
* Cumulative revenue percentage
* Revenue contribution category
* Customer value category

### Customer Value Classification

|    Total Revenue | Customer Value Category |
| ---------------: | ----------------------- |
|  ₹10,000 or more | High-Value Customer     |
| ₹5,000–₹9,999.99 | Medium-Value Customer   |
| ₹1,000–₹4,999.99 | Standard-Value Customer |
|     Below ₹1,000 | Low-Value Customer      |

# ⚠️ Analysis Limitations

This analysis is based on **historical sales data** and rule-based classifications.

The revenue contribution classifications and customer value thresholds are analytical rules defined for this project.

They should not be interpreted as statistically validated customer-value predictions.

The analysis also focuses on revenue contribution and does not directly measure:

* Profitability
* Customer acquisition cost
* Customer lifetime value
* Customer satisfaction
* Marketing cost
* Customer churn probability

These metrics can be analyzed separately.
