# Day 19 – Customer Behavior & Revenue Analytics

## Objective

The objective of Day 19 was to analyze customer purchasing behavior, revenue contribution, order frequency, and customer value using advanced SQL techniques.

## Analysis Completed

- Customer revenue analysis
- Customer order frequency
- Average Order Value
- Customer segmentation
- One-time vs repeat customers
- High-value customer analysis
- Customer revenue ranking
- Revenue contribution analysis
- Top 5 customer analysis
- Overall customer performance reporting

## SQL Concepts Used

- JOIN
- GROUP BY
- HAVING
- CASE
- Subqueries
- CTEs
- Aggregate Functions
- Window Functions
- DENSE_RANK()
- ROUND()
- COUNT()
- SUM()
- AVG()

## Business Insights

The analysis helps identify:

- High-value customers
- Repeat customers
- One-time customers
- Customers generating the highest revenue
- Customer contribution to total revenue
- Average spending per order
- Overall customer performance

## Outcome

Day 19 successfully extended the project from basic sales reporting to customer-level business analytics and revenue intelligence.
