# Day 26 – Customer Revenue Contribution Analysis

## Objective

The objective of Day 26 was to analyze customer revenue contribution and identify high-value customers using advanced SQL techniques.

## SQL Concepts Used

- Common Table Expressions (CTEs)
- Aggregate Functions
- Window Functions
- RANK()
- NTILE()
- SUM() OVER()
- CASE statements
- Revenue contribution analysis
- Customer segmentation
- Business KPI analysis

## Analysis Performed

### 1. Customer Revenue

Calculated the total revenue generated by each customer.

### 2. Customer Revenue Ranking

Ranked customers based on total revenue using the RANK() window function.

### 3. Revenue Contribution

Calculated each customer's percentage contribution to overall revenue.

### 4. Cumulative Revenue

Calculated cumulative revenue and cumulative revenue contribution.

### 5. Customer Segmentation

Customers were classified into:

- High Value
- Medium Value
- Low Value

### 6. Top Revenue Customers

Used NTILE() to identify the top revenue group of customers.

### 7. Business KPI Summary

Calculated:

- Total Customers
- Total Revenue
- Average Customer Revenue
- Highest Customer Revenue
- Lowest Customer Revenue

## Business Insights

The analysis helps identify customers who contribute significantly to overall business revenue.

High-value customers can be targeted for:

- Customer retention campaigns
- Loyalty programs
- Personalized offers
- Premium services
- Cross-selling and upselling
