# 📊 Day 33 — Customer-Product Purchase Analysis

## 📌 Overview

Day 33 focuses on **Customer-Product Purchase Analysis** using MySQL.

The objective of this analysis is to understand the relationship between customers and the products they purchase. By connecting customer orders with order items and product information, the analysis identifies purchasing patterns, product preferences, purchase frequency, customer-product revenue, product diversity, and high-value customer-product relationships.

This analysis extends the customer and product analytics performed in previous days and provides a more detailed view of **customer-level product purchasing behavior**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of Day 33 are:

* Map customers to the products they purchase.
* Measure customer purchase frequency by product.
* Analyze customer spending on individual products.
* Identify the number of unique products purchased by each customer.
* Identify the top product for each customer.
* Identify customers purchasing multiple products.
* Classify customers based on product diversity.
* Rank customer-product relationships by revenue.
* Classify customers based on product purchase frequency.
* Identify high-value customer-product relationships.
* Identify the top customer-product relationships.
* Generate an overall business summary.

# 🔍 Analysis Performed

## 1. Customer-Product Purchase Mapping

The first analysis establishes a direct relationship between customers and the products they purchased.

The analysis calculates:

* Customer ID
* Product ID
* Product name
* Total units purchased
* Total revenue

Revenue is calculated using:

```text
Quantity × Product Price
```

### Business Purpose

This helps identify exactly which products each customer purchases and how much revenue each customer-product relationship generates.

---

## 2. Customer Product Purchase Frequency

This analysis measures how frequently each customer purchases a particular product.

The query calculates:

* Customer ID
* Product ID
* Product name
* Number of purchase orders
* Total units purchased

### Business Purpose

Purchase frequency helps identify:

* Frequent product buyers
* Repeat customers
* Products with strong repeat demand
* Potential opportunities for personalized promotions

---

## 3. Customer Spending by Product

This analysis measures the total revenue generated by each customer for individual products.

### Business Purpose

It helps identify:

* Products generating high customer-level revenue
* High-spending customer-product relationships
* Products preferred by valuable customers

This information can support personalized offers and customer-specific product recommendations.

---

## 4. Unique Products Purchased by Each Customer

The analysis calculates the number of distinct products purchased by every customer.

### Business Purpose

This helps measure **customer product diversity**.

Customers purchasing only one product may have a narrow product preference, while customers purchasing several products demonstrate broader engagement with the product portfolio.

---

## 5. Top Product for Each Customer

A `RANK()` window function is used to rank products according to revenue within each customer.

The highest-ranked product represents the customer's top product based on revenue.

### SQL Concept Used

```sql
RANK() OVER (
    PARTITION BY customer_id
    ORDER BY total_revenue DESC
)
```

### Business Purpose

This helps identify the strongest product preference for each customer.

The information can be used for:

* Personalized recommendations
* Targeted marketing
* Cross-selling
* Customer-specific promotions

---

## 6. Customers Purchasing Multiple Products

This analysis identifies customers who have purchased more than one distinct product.

### Business Purpose

Customers purchasing multiple products are potentially more engaged with the business's product portfolio.

These customers may represent opportunities for:

* Cross-selling
* Upselling
* Product bundles
* Loyalty campaigns

---

## 7. Customer Product Diversity Classification

Customers are classified according to the number of different products they purchase.

### Classification

| Product Count | Segment                 |
| ------------: | ----------------------- |
|             1 | Single Product Customer |
|           2–3 | Multi-Product Customer  |
|   More than 3 | Highly Diverse Customer |

### Business Purpose

This segmentation helps businesses understand the breadth of customer purchasing behavior.

It can also support targeted strategies for moving single-product customers toward multi-product purchasing.

---

## 8. Customer-Product Revenue Ranking

Customer-product combinations are ranked according to their total revenue contribution.

The analysis uses the SQL window function:

```sql
RANK() OVER (
    ORDER BY total_revenue DESC
)
```

### Business Purpose

This identifies the most valuable customer-product relationships across the entire dataset.

High-ranking relationships can be analyzed for:

* Customer loyalty
* Product preference
* Revenue concentration
* Retention opportunities

---

## 9. Customer-Product Purchase Classification

Customers are classified according to how frequently they purchase a particular product.

### Classification

| Order Count | Segment        |
| ----------: | -------------- |
|           1 | One-Time Buyer |
|           2 | Repeat Buyer   |
|   3 or more | Frequent Buyer |

### Business Purpose

This classification helps distinguish between different purchasing behaviors.

It can be useful for:

* Retention campaigns
* Repeat-purchase promotions
* Loyalty programs
* Personalized marketing

---

## 10. High-Value Customer-Product Relationships

Customer-product combinations are classified according to revenue.

### Classification

|         Revenue | Segment      |
| --------------: | ------------ |
| ₹10,000 or more | High Value   |
|   ₹5,000–₹9,999 | Medium Value |
|    Below ₹5,000 | Low Value    |

### Business Purpose

This identifies customer-product relationships that generate significant revenue.

High-value relationships can receive additional attention through:

* Personalized offers
* Loyalty programs
* Premium customer service
* Retention strategies

---

## 11. Top 10 Customer-Product Relationships

The analysis identifies the top 10 customer-product combinations based on total revenue.

### Business Purpose

This provides a quick view of the strongest revenue-generating relationships in the dataset.

These relationships can help management understand where significant customer-product value is concentrated.

---

## 12. Customer-Product Business Summary

The final query provides an overall summary of the customer-product analysis.

The summary includes:

* Total customer-product relationships
* Total customers
* Total products
* Total units purchased
* Total revenue

### Business Purpose

This provides a high-level overview of customer-product activity and summarizes the scale and revenue generated through customer-product relationships.
