## 🎯 Objective

The objective of Day 31 is to analyze **customer purchasing behavior at the product level**.

The analysis examines how products perform based on order frequency, customer reach, units sold, revenue contribution, repeat purchases, and overall product performance.

The goal is to identify products that have strong customer demand and revenue contribution and generate business insights that can support product recommendations, cross-selling, upselling, product targeting, and marketing decisions.

# 🔍 Analysis Performed

## 1. Product Purchase Summary

Analyzed each product using:

* Total orders
* Unique customers
* Units sold
* Total revenue

This provides an overall view of product performance.

---

## 2. Product Customer Reach

Calculated the number of unique customers who purchased each product.

This helps identify products with broad customer demand.

Products purchased by more customers can be considered strong candidates for marketing and promotional campaigns.

---

## 3. Product Purchase Frequency

Measured how frequently products were purchased.

The analysis calculates:

* Number of purchases
* Total units sold
* Average quantity per purchase

This helps identify products with consistently high purchasing activity.

---

## 4. Repeat Purchase Products

Identified products that were purchased across multiple orders.

This helps understand which products demonstrate recurring customer demand.

Repeat-purchase products can be useful for:

* Customer retention campaigns
* Product recommendations
* Subscription opportunities
* Cross-selling strategies

---

## 5. Product Revenue Contribution

Calculated each product's percentage contribution to overall product revenue.

This identifies the products responsible for a significant share of total business revenue.

---

## 6. Product Revenue Ranking

Ranked products according to their total revenue using the `RANK()` window function.

This provides a clear hierarchy of product revenue performance.

---

## 7. Product Performance Classification

Products were classified according to their revenue performance:

* **High Performer**
* **Medium Performer**
* **Low Performer**

This classification provides a simple business-friendly way to evaluate product performance.

---

## 8. Product Customer Reach Ranking

Products were ranked according to the number of unique customers purchasing them.

This helps identify products with the strongest customer reach.

---

## 9. Product Sales Segmentation

Products were segmented according to units sold:

* **High Volume**
* **Medium Volume**
* **Low Volume**

This provides another perspective on product demand beyond revenue.

---

## 10. Product Business Summary

Created a consolidated product-level business analysis containing:

* Total orders
* Unique customers
* Units sold
* Total revenue
* Revenue rank
* Revenue contribution
* Performance category

This acts as a product-level business report for decision-making.

# 📈 Key Business Metrics

| Metric               | Description                                          |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Total Orders         | Number of orders containing the product              |
| Unique Customers     | Number of distinct customers purchasing the product  |
| Units Sold           | Total quantity sold                                  |
| Total Revenue        | Revenue generated by the product                     |
| Purchase Frequency   | Number of purchase occurrences                       |
| Average Quantity     | Average quantity purchased per transaction           |
| Revenue Contribution | Percentage of total revenue generated by the product |
| Revenue Rank         | Product position based on revenue                    |
| Customer Reach Rank  | Product position based on unique customers           |
| Performance Category | High, Medium, or Low Performer                       |
| Sales Volume Segment | High, Medium, or Low Volume                          |
