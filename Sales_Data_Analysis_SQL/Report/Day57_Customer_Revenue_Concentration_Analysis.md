## 📈 Revenue Contribution

Customer revenue contribution is calculated using:

```text
Customer Revenue Contribution %

= Customer Revenue / Total Revenue × 100
```

The analysis uses:

```sql
SUM(total_revenue) OVER ()
```

to calculate total revenue across the customer result set.

---

## 📊 Cumulative Revenue Contribution

Customers are ordered from highest to lowest revenue.

The cumulative revenue percentage shows how quickly total revenue accumulates as customers are added according to their revenue ranking.

This helps identify:

* Major revenue contributors
* Core revenue customers
* Long-tail customers
* Revenue concentration patterns

---

## 🏆 Revenue Concentration Segments

The analysis uses the following business-analysis classifications:

| Cumulative Revenue | Classification             |
| -----------------: | -------------------------- |
|          Up to 20% | Top Revenue Contributors   |
|            20%–50% | Major Revenue Contributors |
|            50%–80% | Core Revenue Customers     |
|          Above 80% | Long-Tail Customers        |

These classifications are rule-based analytical categories created for this portfolio project.

---

## 🔢 Top 20% Customer Analysis

`NTILE(5)` is used to divide customers into five approximately equal groups.

The first group represents the highest-revenue customer quintile.

```sql
NTILE(5) OVER (
    ORDER BY total_revenue DESC
)
```

The analysis then calculates the total revenue generated by this top customer group.

## ⚠️ Methodology Limitation

This analysis is a **descriptive and rule-based business analysis**.

The Pareto-style analysis does not assume that exactly 20% of customers will always generate exactly 80% of revenue.

The actual revenue distribution depends on the underlying sales data.
