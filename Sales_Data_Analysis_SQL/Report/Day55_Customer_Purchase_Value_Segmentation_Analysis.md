# 📊 Day 55 — Customer Purchase Value Segmentation Analysis

## 📌 Overview

Day 55 focuses on **Customer Purchase Value Segmentation Analysis** using MySQL.

The objective is to analyze customers based on their purchase value, order frequency, units purchased, average order value, revenue contribution, and revenue ranking.

The analysis uses SQL-based business rules to divide customers into:

* High-Value Customers
* Medium-Value Customers
* Low-Value Customers

This analysis helps understand which customers contribute the most revenue and provides a foundation for customer-focused business strategies.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of Day 55 are:

1. Calculate total revenue generated by each customer.
2. Calculate total orders placed by each customer.
3. Calculate average order value for each customer.
4. Calculate total units purchased by each customer.
5. Classify customers based on purchase frequency.
6. Calculate each customer's contribution to total revenue.
7. Rank customers according to revenue.
8. Segment customers based on purchase value.
9. Identify high-value customers.
10. Identify medium-value customers.
11. Identify low-value customers.
12. Create a final customer value segmentation summary.

## 💰 Revenue Calculation

Customer revenue is calculated using:

```text
Revenue = Quantity × Product Price
```

SQL implementation:

```sql
oi.quantity * p.price
```

The product price is taken from the `products` table.

# 📊 Analysis Performed

## 1. Total Revenue per Customer

Calculates the total revenue generated by each customer.

### Business Purpose

Helps identify customers who generate higher revenue for the business.

---

## 2. Total Orders per Customer

Calculates the number of orders placed by each customer.

### Business Purpose

Helps understand customer purchasing frequency and engagement.

---

## 3. Average Order Value per Customer

Calculates the average value of orders placed by each customer.

### Business Purpose

Helps identify customers who tend to make larger purchases per order.

---

## 4. Total Units Purchased per Customer

Calculates the total number of product units purchased by each customer.

### Business Purpose

Helps measure customer purchasing volume.

---

## 5. Customer Purchase Frequency

Customers are classified based on the number of orders:

| Orders | Classification         |
| -----: | ---------------------- |
|    10+ | Very Frequent Customer |
|    5–9 | Frequent Customer      |
|    2–4 | Occasional Customer    |
|      1 | One-Time Customer      |

### Business Purpose

This classification helps understand how frequently customers interact with the business.

---

## 6. Customer Revenue Contribution

Calculates each customer's percentage contribution to total business revenue.

### Formula

```text
Revenue Contribution % =
Customer Revenue / Total Revenue × 100
```

### SQL Technique

The analysis uses a window function:

```sql
SUM(total_revenue) OVER ()
```

### Business Purpose

Helps determine how much each customer contributes to overall revenue.

---

## 7. Customer Revenue Ranking

Customers are ranked according to their total revenue.

The analysis uses:

```sql
RANK() OVER (
    ORDER BY total_revenue DESC
)
```

### Business Purpose

Provides a revenue-based ranking of customers and helps identify the highest-revenue customers.

---

# 🏆 Customer Value Segmentation

Customers are segmented using the following rule-based classification:

|   Total Revenue | Customer Segment      |
| --------------: | --------------------- |
| ₹10,000 or more | High-Value Customer   |
|   ₹5,000–₹9,999 | Medium-Value Customer |
|    Below ₹5,000 | Low-Value Customer    |

These thresholds are business rules defined for this SQL portfolio analysis.

---

## ⭐ 8. Customer Value Classification

Each customer is assigned a value segment based on total revenue.

### Classification Logic

```sql
CASE
    WHEN total_revenue >= 10000
        THEN 'High-Value Customer'
    WHEN total_revenue >= 5000
        THEN 'Medium-Value Customer'
    ELSE 'Low-Value Customer'
END
```

### Business Purpose

Provides a simple customer-value framework for business analysis.

---

## 💎 9. High-Value Customer Identification

Customers generating **₹10,000 or more** in revenue are classified as:

**High-Value Customers**

This analysis isolates customers belonging to the highest revenue segment.

---

## 🟡 10. Medium-Value Customer Identification

Customers generating between **₹5,000 and ₹9,999** are classified as:

**Medium-Value Customers**

This segment represents customers with moderate purchase value under the defined rules.

---

## 🔵 11. Low-Value Customer Identification

Customers generating **below ₹5,000** are classified as:

**Low-Value Customers**

This segment contains customers with comparatively lower purchase value under the defined segmentation rules.

# ⚠️ Methodology Limitation

The customer-value segmentation used in Day 55 is a **rule-based SQL classification**.

It is not:

* A machine-learning model
* A statistically validated segmentation model
* A probability prediction
* An automatically optimized segmentation system

The revenue thresholds were defined as business-analysis rules for this portfolio project.
