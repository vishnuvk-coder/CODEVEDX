# 📊 Day 20 – Customer Retention & Repeat Purchase Analysis

## Project

Sales Data Analysis using SQL

---

## Objective

The objective of Day 20 was to analyze customer purchasing behavior and identify repeat customers, one-time customers, customer revenue contribution, and high-value customers.

---

## Analysis Performed

### 1. Customer Order Frequency

Calculated the number of orders placed by each customer.

### 2. Customer Classification

Customers were classified into:

- One-Time Customers
- Repeat Customers

### 3. Customer Type Summary

Calculated the total number of one-time and repeat customers.

### 4. Repeat Customer Rate

Calculated the percentage of customers who placed more than one order.

### 5. Customer Revenue Analysis

Calculated customer-level revenue using:

Revenue = Quantity × Product Price

### 6. High-Value Repeat Customers

Identified customers who placed multiple orders and generated significant revenue.

### 7. Average Order Value

Calculated the average revenue generated per order for each customer.

### 8. Top Customers

Identified customers with:

- Highest revenue
- Highest order frequency

### 9. Customer Segmentation

Customers were categorized based on their purchasing frequency:

- One-Time Customer
- Repeat Customer
- Highly Engaged Customer

### 10. Revenue Contribution

Calculated each customer's percentage contribution to total revenue using SQL Window Functions.

### 11. Retention Summary

Generated a final summary containing:

- Total customers
- One-time customers
- Repeat customers
- Repeat customer rate

## Business Insights

Customer retention analysis helps a business understand purchasing behavior and identify valuable customers.

Repeat customers can be targeted through:

- Loyalty programs
- Personalized offers
- Cross-selling
- Upselling
- Customer retention campaigns
- Targeted promotions

High-value repeat customers are particularly important because they combine purchasing frequency with strong revenue contribution.
