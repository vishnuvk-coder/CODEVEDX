## 📊 ABC Classification

Customers are classified according to cumulative revenue contribution:

| Cumulative Revenue | Segment |
|---|---|
| Up to 80% | A |
| 80%–95% | B |
| Above 95% | C |

These thresholds are used as the analytical framework for this portfolio project.

---
