# Day 22 Report – RFM Customer Segmentation Analysis

## Project

Sales Data Analysis Using SQL

## Objective

The objective of Day 22 was to perform RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) customer segmentation using MySQL.

The analysis identifies customer purchasing behavior and categorizes customers based on their purchasing recency, order frequency, and total monetary contribution.

---

## RFM Analysis

### Recency

Measures how recently a customer placed an order.

### Frequency

Measures the number of unique orders placed by a customer.

### Monetary

Measures the total revenue generated by a customer.

## Analysis Performed

### 1. Customer RFM Metrics

Calculated:

- Last order date
- Recency
- Order frequency
- Monetary value

### 2. RFM Scoring

Customers were assigned scores from 1 to 5 for:

- Recency
- Frequency
- Monetary value

### 3. Customer Segmentation

Customers were categorized into segments such as:

- Champions
- Loyal Customers
- New Customers
- At Risk Customers
- High Value At Risk
- Regular Customers

### 4. Revenue Contribution

Calculated the percentage contribution of each customer to total revenue.

### 5. Customer Ranking

Ranked customers based on:

- Revenue
- Order frequency
- Customer value

### 6. Customer Lifetime Value

Calculated customer lifetime value and average order value.
