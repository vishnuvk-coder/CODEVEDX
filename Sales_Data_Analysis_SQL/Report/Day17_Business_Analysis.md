# 📊 Day 17 – Business Analysis & Insights

## Business Insights

### 1. Overall Revenue
Total revenue represents the overall sales generated by the business.

### 2. Average Order Value
Average Order Value (AOV) shows the average revenue generated from each order.

### 3. Top Customers
Top customers identify the customers contributing the highest revenue to the business.

### 4. Top Products
Top products identify the products generating the highest revenue and help determine the strongest-performing products.

### 5. Category Performance
Category-level revenue helps identify the strongest-performing product categories.

### 6. Order Status Analysis
Order status analysis helps understand the distribution of completed, pending, and cancelled orders.

## 📌 Conclusion

The analysis provides a business-oriented view of sales performance by examining revenue, customers, products, categories, and order status.

These insights can support better decision-making related to sales strategy, customer management, and product performance.
