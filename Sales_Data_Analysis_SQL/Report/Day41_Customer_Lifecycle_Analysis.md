# 📊 Day 41 — Customer Lifecycle & Repeat Purchase Analysis

## 1. Introduction

Day 41 focuses on understanding the customer purchasing lifecycle and repeat-purchase behavior.

The analysis tracks customers from their first purchase through repeat purchases and classifies customers according to their purchasing frequency.

The objective is to understand customer engagement, repeat purchasing, loyalty, purchase intervals, and revenue contribution.

---

# 2. Objectives

The main objectives of Day 41 are:

* Identify each customer's first purchase.
* Identify customers who made a second purchase.
* Calculate the time taken to make a second purchase.
* Calculate the repeat purchase rate.
* Identify one-time customers.
* Analyze customer purchase frequency.
* Calculate purchase intervals.
* Classify customers into lifecycle stages.
* Analyze revenue generated by repeat customers.
* Compare repeat and one-time customers.
* Identify loyal customers.
* Rank customers according to lifecycle engagement.
* Create a final customer lifecycle business summary.

# 4. Customer Lifecycle Concept

Customer lifecycle analysis studies how customers behave over time.

A simplified lifecycle can be represented as:

```text
First Purchase
      ↓
Second Purchase
      ↓
Developing Customer
      ↓
Loyal Customer
      ↓
Highly Engaged Customer
```

Some customers may remain one-time customers and require re-engagement strategies.

---

# 5. Analysis 01 — Customer First Purchase

The first analysis identifies the earliest purchase date for every customer.

### SQL Concept

```text
MIN(order_date)
```

### Business Purpose

This establishes the starting point of each customer's lifecycle.

---

# 6. Analysis 02 — Customer Second Purchase

The second analysis identifies customers who returned after their first purchase.

### SQL Concepts

* `ROW_NUMBER()`
* `PARTITION BY`
* `ORDER BY`

### Business Purpose

Customers making a second purchase demonstrate initial repeat-purchase behavior.

---

# 7. Analysis 03 — Days to Second Purchase

This analysis calculates the number of days between the first and second purchase.

### SQL Concept

```text
DATEDIFF()
```

### Business Purpose

A shorter purchase gap can indicate faster customer conversion into repeat purchasing.

---

# 8. Analysis 04 — Repeat Purchase Rate

This analysis calculates:

* Total customers
* Repeat customers
* One-time customers
* Repeat purchase rate
* One-time customer rate

### Business Purpose

Repeat purchase rate is an important customer-retention KPI.

A higher repeat purchase rate generally indicates stronger customer engagement.

---

# 9. Analysis 05 — Customer Purchase Frequency

Customers are classified according to their total number of orders.

| Orders | Lifecycle Stage |
| -----: | --------------- |
|      1 | One-Time        |
|    2–3 | Developing      |
|    4–6 | Loyal           |
|     7+ | Highly Engaged  |

These thresholds are business-analysis rules and can be adjusted depending on the business.

---

# 10. Analysis 06 — Purchase Interval Analysis

This analysis calculates the number of days between consecutive purchases.

### SQL Concepts

* `LAG()`
* `DATEDIFF()`
* Window Functions

### Business Purpose

Purchase intervals help identify normal purchasing cycles and customers whose purchasing activity is slowing down.

---

# 11. Analysis 07 — Customer Lifecycle Stage

Each customer is assigned a lifecycle stage based on purchase frequency.

The analysis includes:

* First purchase date
* Last purchase date
* Total orders
* Lifecycle stage

### Business Purpose

This helps businesses understand where customers currently stand in their purchasing lifecycle.

---

# 12. Analysis 08 — Repeat Customer Revenue

This analysis calculates revenue generated by each customer and identifies whether the customer is:

* Repeat Customer
* One-Time Customer

### Business Purpose

It helps determine the financial importance of repeat customers.

---

# 13. Analysis 09 — Repeat vs One-Time Customers

This analysis compares:

* Customer count
* Total revenue
* Average customer revenue

between repeat and one-time customers.

### Business Purpose

This helps determine whether repeat customers contribute a larger share of customer revenue.

---

# 14. Analysis 10 — Loyal Customers

Customers with four or more orders are classified as loyal customers for this analysis.

The analysis identifies:

* Customer ID
* Total orders
* First purchase date
* Last purchase date

### Business Purpose

These customers can be considered candidates for loyalty programs and retention campaigns.

---

# 15. Analysis 11 — Customer Lifecycle Ranking

Customers are ranked according to their total number of purchases.

### SQL Concept

```text
RANK()
```

### Business Purpose

This identifies highly engaged customers and allows the business to prioritize its most active customers.

---

# 16. Analysis 12 — Final Customer Lifecycle Business Summary

The final analysis combines:

* Customer ID
* Total orders
* First purchase date
* Last purchase date
* Customer lifetime
* Total revenue
* Lifecycle stage
* Lifecycle rank

### Business Purpose

This provides a consolidated customer-level view for business decision-making.
